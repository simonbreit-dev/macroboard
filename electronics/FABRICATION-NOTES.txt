MAKROBOARD — TOP-USB 80 x 82 mm — 2026-10-17

Use only the matching TOP-USB revision's Gerbers, drill files, BOM and CPL.
Do not mix with the earlier 80x82mm RIGHT-EDGE-USB revision, the 80x122mm
revision, or earlier via-in-pad artwork. USB-C is now on the TOP edge.

PCB: 80 x 82 mm, 4 layers, nominal finished thickness 1.0mm, FR4, ENIG.
Finished copper target 35um / 1oz on all four layers.
Copper order TOP -> BOTTOM:
  L1 F.Cu: signals / VBUS pour / two Top-side 0805 capacitors C20, C21
  L2 In1.Cu: continuous +3V3 plane; no signal tracks
  L3 In2.Cu: continuous GND reference plane; no signal tracks
  L4 B.Cu: controller, flash, crystal, USB, signals and GND pour

USB DIFFERENTIAL IMPEDANCE
USB D+/D- main pair on B.Cu, reference adjacent In2.Cu GND.
Nominal main width 0.225mm, edge gap 0.150mm; target 90 ohms +/-10%.
The B.Cu copper-pour keepout corridor along the main USB route is intentional.
Component pads, ESD ground and connector contact duplication are local
non-uniform transitions, not an impedance-controlled microstrip section.

Nominal stackup TOP -> BOTTOM (mm):

Actual material, resin content, dielectric/copper thickness and mask vary.

BEFORE PRODUCTION: confirm the actual stackup and calculate the 90-ohm pair
with your field solver. Supply the controlled-impedance calculation/coupon
verification. Do not silently substitute 1.6mm thickness or change widths in
CAM. The approx. 89.7-ohm first-order estimate is not a field-solver result,
measurement or USB certification. If the actual stackup cannot achieve the
target with this geometry, an engineering revision is required before making.

Minimum routed track width 0.127mm; copper clearance 0.150mm.
Ordinary through vias: 0.450/0.200mm and 0.500/0.250mm diameter/drill.
149 vias, ALL tented on BOTH outer faces. NO vias in solder pads.
No resin fill or copper capping is requested or necessary for this artwork.
U4 exposed ground land is solid copper without drilled vias; its four local
ground vias lie outside the solder land. Preserve mask/paste windowing.
Do not tent component plated contacts or USB shell anchors.

Separate PTH/NPTH Excellon files supplied. The internal LED apertures in
Edge.Cuts and the switch shaft holes are intentional. Preserve routed slots.
All four copper layers are required. F.Paste/B.Paste are stencil artwork.
